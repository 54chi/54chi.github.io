In 2016, Sachi decided to take a leap year (aka: quit his job). Tired with the status quo of the IT consulting world ([some examples here](http://www.doodlekit.com/blog/entry/55860/top-10-reasons-why-corporate-it-sucks)), he is planning to rethink the way enterprise IT should operate in the modern world, while at the same time doing some upgrading to his skills and travel around the World to [prevent this](https://medium.com/@mutlu82/why-talented-creatives-are-leaving-your-shitty-agency-6f4ec6f70fbe#.n6hzl9bjl).

At least that's the theory. Sachi is addicted to coffee shops, and decent internet speed...and while he planned a small budget to do this crazy plan, it is not going to last forever...unless he finds gold in one of his expeditions (and/or is self-financed by one if his projects ;))

Sachi is not a professional blogger, and this site is most likely not going to be promoted outside of his circle of friends. The main purpose of is to serve as a log for this year long Journey, and what happens by the end of it (and show off what I can do by myself).

The opinions expressed in this site (specially in the comics section ^^;;) are mine and mine alone. They do not reflect the opinions of my employers (past or present), co-workers, family, pets, or possibly even me if the post you’re reading is more than a few days old.
