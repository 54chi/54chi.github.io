I confess I'm an avid manga/anime consumer. It's a good escape valve, and I get to practice some Japanese. Sometimes I learn other things from them too.

One of those things have been the concept of **"slow life"**: the protagonist is fed up of his/her job and switch into a simpler life (sometimes in another world), like crafting, farming, etc. abandoning the stresses of modern life. 

For years now, I've been pretty grumpy. While the corporate job pays very well, I couldn't help but getting more and more frustrated at the lack of people trying to do a good job. Either because they expect someone else to cover for them or -worse- that nobody notice. Call it quiet quitting, or call it laziness, I was never good at neither of them and feel bad at getting paid for sitting around pretending to be busy. I noticed I had been projecting my frustrations on others,  and wanted to correct that, fully knowing that the answer was looking for another job (within or outside the company).

Then I got the push I needed, thanks to a recent announcement from the company, and instead of looking for another job immediately, I'll be slowing down in life (or so I am proclaiming). In other words: I'm going to experiment taking a long long long vacation from the corporate world and try to learn and enjoy from "other worlds", improve the skills that I always wanted and test slow living my way. I wonder what I'll see in the mirror a few weeks from now. Right now is like being dropped in the middle of the forest with a survival kit, but no map.

I don't expect to become a farmer -at least not yet-, but when it comes to "crafting", I've always supported the [Software Craftsmanship Manifesto ](https://manifesto.softwarecraftsmanship.org/) . Now that we are surrounded with AI-something everywhere, is probably wise to check "revised" versions of the manifest like [here](https://ai-manifesto.software-craftsmanship.dev/principles), and [here](https://ericsilva.org/posts/ai-manifesto/), before deciding which path to follow.

I'll be setting a soft schedule for a few of the projects I have in mind. With the help of AI, YouTube and some luck this may work well. Let this blog and following posts become my trail blazing.
