**Progress:** I'm already off course lol. My original GTD premise was simple: 3 days for a "deep focus", to work on personal LLM-assisted projects (likely one big and 2 smaller ones), then the other two for lighter tasks, including blogging.

But after day 1, I was obsessed, do have gone over the original budget. 

The plan for the next 2-4 weeks was to come up with a high level architecture for what counts as a "country". Then prioritize the modules that can be implemented across all types of organizations first (so can be challenged as a smaller scale -- like a company size maybe?) and start working for an MVP that may be gamified.

![[2026-08-27-Week one of testing new GTD system-1787855105644.webp]]

5 days after I wrote that I have devolved (evolved?) into a research on what a "better" country would be. No need to spend much time defining countries that nobody wanted to belong to (e.g. monarchies, dictatorships, etc.) and more of understanding why the current world exists. So I did a bit of history, and a bit of politics, and a bit of economics. I ran a bunch of ideas with Claude, and ended up inspired to write about that. Then I decided that explaining it in a book or essay may be too boring so made up characters to live in it. And then I took it further: what if this was a choose-your-own-adventure sort of book, add a few twists and made it hard scifi?

Explaining the country this way turned out harder than expected, but it has been fun. And thanks to Claude and HugginFace, the progress is incredible, working as thesaurus that adapts to my style and make sure I'm not conflicting my characters stories within the timeline. The main plot is ready but I need to develop the scenarios and the characters better. Also, not super clear on how I wan to gamify this yet. 

Anyways, TL;DR: Need a better discipline for the GTD lol.
