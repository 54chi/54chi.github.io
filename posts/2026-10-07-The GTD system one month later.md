**Progress:** I've divided my day into few routines (that I am trying to convert into habits):
- Mornings: stretch, take vitamins, prepare the day, etc.
- Work: take a break at the same time always. That's it.
- Afternoon: write the review of the day, plan for the next one, go workout
- Night: stop watching screens, mental review, sleep early.

It is more detailed than above, but so far so good (3 weeks in). I still struggle to go to bed "on time", and take the work break, but I can see myself starting to form the habit of working out.

**Insight:** postponing lunch and put it between work and workout time, with a little walk in between to help digest
