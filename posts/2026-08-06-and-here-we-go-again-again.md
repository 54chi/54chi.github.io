### Quick update on projects

I am also riding the AI wave, tho my motivation is not to get right (at least not yet lol).

Currently experimenting with "meta skills" (or whatever is called), which is basically using a customized markdown to define parameters and then use a generic skill that can use the context previously defined by the markdown.

This makes a lot more sense when you work in a team and each one of them uses a collection of skills (E.g. for reviews, reports, building, etc.) that are very similar but need to be adapted since they don't share the same role. 

So, is more as a way to fix the problems from groups of people not sharing standards, tho in reality is mostly because their parameters are different, but not the actual work (or whatever the skills are supposed to do). It does require better prompt engineer, but when it works wells it accomplish the following:

1. Teams are still able to adapt the skills to their own needs, even tho they are using the same skills across the org.
2. The metaskill (customized markdown), helps understand how they see and define the world in an objective manner (or at least closer to the lazy "it depends" traditional mentality).
3. And by depending on AI, the context constraint becomes a good way to keep the language as precise as possible.
